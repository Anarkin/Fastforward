# FEATURES-CANVAS

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

- What a canvas can do, and how much of it Fastforward's canvas does: a board of notes and the edges between them, kept as JSON Canvas, the open format of Obsidian's Canvas

- Fastforward: ✅ Yes, 🟡 Partial, ❌ No

- Tier, a judgement call from what Obsidian's Canvas, Excalidraw, tldraw, Miro, FigJam and Heptabase offer: Core is in nearly every canvas, Common in most, Advanced in a few, Niche in one or two, if any

- The key is the one Obsidian's Canvas uses for the feature, as JSON Canvas comes from it; where Fastforward uses another, the notes say so

- Every attribute of JSON Canvas 1.0 has a row, named in its notes in `code`, so the table tells what of the format the canvas reads, shows and edits

- What the canvas shares with every tool, the tabs and windows, the keyboard, the menus, the lists, the look and the settings of the app, [FEATURES](FEATURES.md#common-app) says

## Files

| Feature | What it does | Key | Tier | Fastforward | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open a canvas | Open a file of a canvas | — | Core | ✅ Yes | Open with Canvas in the menu of a file named .canvas, in any case, on disk, in the commander or on a part of a path anywhere, in a canvas tab of its own named after the file, with the icon of the canvas, or the tab drawing it already; its path above the canvas, from the top left of its nodes, or why it draws none, a file over 50 MB, of no JSON or of JSON with no canvas; not inside an archive; viewed or edited as JSON, colored so |
| JSON Canvas | Read the open format of JSON Canvas 1.0 | — | Core | ✅ Yes | Every attribute the spec defines, nodes of text, files, links and groups, the subpath of a file and the label and background of a group, and the edges between their sides, with their ends, colors and labels, in the order of the file, the first lowest; canvases as Obsidian writes them, a tab an indent and an object a line, and fields Obsidian's plugins add, tested; a node or an edge it can't read left out rather than the file, a notice counting them once while its tab is open; an empty file, or a byte order mark before the JSON, read as a canvas |
| Restore after a restart | Open the canvases again once the app starts | — | Common | ✅ Yes | Their tabs come back, read anew |
| Follow changes | Draw a canvas anew once its file changes | — | Common | ✅ Yes | Watched while its tab shows, as the editor watches its file, and drawn anew once another program changes it while nothing of it is unsaved; its edits kept otherwise, a notice saying that saving writes over the change; its own save never taken for a change; read anew each time its tab shows too |
| Save | Write the canvas | Ctrl+S | Core | ✅ Yes | Mod+S, through a file of its own beside it that replaces it once whole, as the editor saves; written as its file was, as Obsidian writes a canvas, a tab an indent and an object a line, or indented or on one line, with its line endings, byte order mark and last line break, every field and node it could not read kept, so a canvas saved unchanged is written byte for byte and an edit changes the lines of what it changed alone; a star on its tab while it has changes not saved, closing it asking first; a file read-only refused, saying so; held in the main process, so a reload or a move to another window keeps its edits |
| New canvas | Make a canvas | — | Core | ✅ Yes | Shift+F4 in the commander with a name ending in .canvas, in any case, opens an empty canvas in a canvas tab of its own, its file written only once saved, as `{}`, though nothing was added to it, as the editor makes a new file; a canvas whose file is not there is drawn empty the same way |

## Board

| Feature | What it does | Key | Tier | Fastforward | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Nodes | Draw the nodes of a canvas | — | Core | ✅ Yes | `nodes`, each with its `id`, `type`, `x`, `y`, `width` and `height` in pixels; as boxes of box-drawing characters on the cells of the monospace font, drawn by the canvas on whole device pixels so their lines meet from row to row, a node over those before it in the file, one smaller than its borders a line or □; the selected node in double lines, once nodes can be selected |
| Grid | Snap the nodes to a grid | — | Common | ✅ Yes | Shown as a dot of one stroke where four cells meet, dimmed, hidden under nodes and seen through groups; each node on the cells its edges are nearest, a cell as many pixels of the file as the cell of the monospace font at 100%, in whole pixels, 7 by 18 by default, so a canvas looks otherwise with another font; a node moved, sized or placed written on whole cells; the view settling on whole cells once panned |
| Pan | Move around the canvas | Space+Drag | Core | ✅ Yes | Space+drag and Middle-drag following the pointer, a touchpad both ways at once by the pixels it strokes, the wheel the rows of `scrolling.wheelRows` a notch and Shift+Wheel as many columns, gliding there with `scrolling.smooth`; each settling on whole cells once it rests or is let go, unless `scrolling.smoothSnap` is off; starting at the top left of the nodes, and kept with its tab as it is shown again; no scrollbars, as a canvas has no ends |
| Zoom | Make the canvas larger or smaller | Ctrl+Wheel | Core | ❌ No | To come later, in steps of whole device pixels of a cell |
| Select | Select nodes and edges by a click or a box | — | Core | ✅ Yes | A click on a node, a group by its border, or on an edge, its line or its label, Shift+click or Mod+click putting it in or taking it out, as Excalidraw, tldraw and Figma toggle it; a box dragged over the empty canvas selecting the nodes it touches and the groups it holds whole, with Shift or Mod added to the selection; Mod+A every node; Esc none; drawn in double lines, an edge to its ends, kept with its tab |
| Move and resize | Drag a node, or its sides and corners | — | Core | ✅ Yes | The nodes selected dragged a cell at a time, a group by its top, its label too, with the nodes it holds whole, as Obsidian moves them; a side or a corner of a node selected dragged to size it, but the top of a group, never under two cells, the pointer showing which; drawn as they go, one change once let go |
| Add nodes | Place a new node under the pointer | Mod+1, Mod+2 | Core | 🟡 Partial | Mod+1 a text node of 30 by 5 cells and Mod+2 a group of 40 by 12, following the pointer until a click places it, selected, Esc giving it up, a text node taking what is typed at once; no files and links placed |
| Delete | Delete the nodes and edges selected | Delete | Core | ✅ Yes | Delete and Backspace, the nodes selected with their edges, and the edges selected |
| Undo and redo | Take back and redo changes | Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z | Core | ✅ Yes | Mod+Z, Mod+Shift+Z and Mod+Y, up to 500 changes, what was typed into a node from its start to its end one change, the tab no longer marked unsaved once back where it was saved |

## Nodes and edges

| Feature | What it does | Key | Tier | Fastforward | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Text | Type text in a node | Enter, double click | Core | ✅ Yes | `text` of a node of the type `text`; typed in the text node selected on Enter, where a double click falls, the word under it selected and a third click the line, as the views select them, and in a text node placed at once, through the EditContext of the browser and the input session of the editor, input methods too, what they compose drawn as composed and their window told where each character composed stands on the cells: Backspace an accent alone and Delete a character with its accents, with Ctrl a word, as the editor deletes them, by the same code, Enter a new line, the arrows a character, a word with Ctrl and a row of those it wraps onto, Home and End the row, Mod+Home and Mod+End the text, Shift selecting, Mod+A the whole text, a click placing the cursor and a drag selecting; cut, copied and pasted as plain text; the cursor drawn by the `cursor` settings, as the editor draws it, as wide as the character under it, the selection in its color, the page itself never selecting what a click falls on, the rows scrolled within the node to keep the cursor in sight; written into the canvas as typed, so its tab is marked unsaved at once and Mod+S saves it, a reload or a move to another window bringing it back where it was typed; Escape or a click outside the node ending it, the node selected; drawn by the engine of the views as a file viewed is, a test comparing a node with the viewer pixel by pixel: wrapped as every view wraps a text, a column kept for a blank hanging at the end of a row and one for the mark of its break, whitespace and line ends signed while the views show whitespace, wide characters, emoji and signs on the cells the views give them, cut where its node ends; a file and a link not typed yet |
| Markdown | Show the text of a node rendered | — | Common | 🟡 Partial | Colored on the cells as the viewer colors a Markdown file, by the same grammar in the worker of the colors, its markers, code, links, lists and quotes in the colors of the theme, as typed too, the lines a change leaves alone keeping their colors while the new ones come; emphasis and headings by color rather than weight or size, as the cells draw every character in the one font of the grid; not rendered as the preview renders it |
| Edges | Join two nodes by an arrow from side to side | — | Core | ✅ Yes | `edges`, each with its `id`, `fromNode` and `toNode`; drawn as lines of box-drawing characters from the middle of a side, a joint where they leave a node, turning at right angles; through the nodes in their way rather than around them; drawn by hand by dragging the mark that shows beside the middle of a side of a node while the pointer is in its cell to another node, joining the side of it nearest the pointer once let go, as Obsidian writes an edge, and selected; an end not moved to another node yet |
| Arrows | Point an edge one way, the other way, both ways or neither | A | Core | ✅ Yes | `fromEnd` and `toEnd`, `none` or `arrow`, an arrow drawn at each end that asks for one, none at the start and one at the end where the file names neither, as JSON Canvas takes them; A on the edges selected turns them from pointing at where they go to pointing back, both ways, neither way and round again, from the ends of the first of them, an end written only where it differs from what JSON Canvas takes it to be without one, as Obsidian writes it; Obsidian chooses them from a menu |
| Edge sides | Join an edge to a side of each node | — | Common | 🟡 Partial | `fromSide` and `toSide`, `top`, `right`, `bottom` or `left`, drawn from the middle of the side the file names, the sides that face each other where it names none; chosen as an edge is drawn by hand, the side nearest the pointer; an end not moved to another side yet |
| Edge labels | Write a label on an edge | — | Common | 🟡 Partial | `label` of an edge, shown halfway along it; not written yet |
| Colors | Color nodes and edges | — | Common | ✅ Yes | `color` of a node or an edge, one of the six of JSON Canvas, `1` to `6`, drawn in the colors of the git graph, or a color of its own, `#` and its hex digits; one of the six by its name, or none, chosen for the nodes and edges selected from the Color submenu of the menu a right click opens, the one clicked selected first unless it is selected already, the color they share checked, as one edit undone at once; a color of its own typed as # and six hex digits into the prompt Custom… opens, the prompt kept with its tab, with what was typed, as it is shown again, reloaded or moved |
| Order | Draw the nodes in order, those later over those before | — | Core | ✅ Yes | The order of `nodes`, the first lowest, groups under the rest, as JSON Canvas lists them; a node added placed on top, a group under the rest; Bring to Front and Send to Back in the menu a right click opens move the nodes selected over or under every other, keeping their order among themselves |
| Groups | Gather nodes in a labeled frame | — | Common | ✅ Yes | A node of the type `group`, drawn as a frame the nodes inside it are drawn over, moved with the nodes it holds whole |
| Group labels | Name a group | Enter, double click | Common | ✅ Yes | `label` of a group, drawn in its top border, cut where the border ends; typed on Enter on the group selected or a double click on its label, as the text of a node is typed, Enter or Escape ending it, a line break pasted into it typed as a space, as a label holds one line; not on a group placed, which is moved by its top as it is placed |
| Group backgrounds | Show an image behind a group, covering it, at its ratio or repeated | — | Advanced | ❌ No | `background` and `backgroundStyle`, `cover`, `ratio` or `repeat`, read and kept in the file as they are, not drawn |
| Files | Show a file in a node | — | Common | 🟡 Partial | A node of the type `file`, its `file` path and its `subpath`, a heading or block after its #, shown; not the file |
| Links | Show a web page in a node | Enter, double click | Common | 🟡 Partial | A node of the type `link`, its `url` drawn in the color of a link below the title of its page, which the main process reads from the web over https alone, from the first 256 KB of the page at most, within 10 seconds and without cookies, once a page while the app runs; opened by the system on Enter on the node selected or a double click, through the window, which lets out https and mailto alone, as it opens a link of a Markdown preview; not the page itself, as no page of the web runs in the app |
